GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Who is Fermi designed for?

Small businesses that manage products, stock, sales and invoices, and want to organise those activities in a connected platform.

Are the examples on these pages real business data?

No. Every demonstration on these public pages uses illustrative sample data. They do not access, change or display customer business records.

Does Fermi replace my accounting advice?

No. Operational sales reports help you understand recorded activity. They are not complete accounts, tax advice or a guarantee of profitability.

How do I begin?

Create an account or log in, explore the dashboard, then set up your business profile and product records when you are ready.
